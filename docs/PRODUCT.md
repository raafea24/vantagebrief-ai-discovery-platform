# VantageBrief Product Specification

## Product Identity

**Product name:** VantageBrief

**Product category:** AI Client Discovery and Opportunity Intelligence

**Primary tagline:** Turn business conversations into decision-ready briefs.

**Product description:**

VantageBrief is an AI-powered client discovery workspace that helps users explain a business challenge, clarify their current workflow, identify practical opportunities, and receive a structured, actionable brief by email.

VantageBrief must be presented as a credible business product, not as a general-purpose chatbot, experimental AI interface, student assignment, or basic contact form.

The conversational interface is only one part of the experience. The actual product value comes from understanding business context, structuring requirements, identifying next steps, and converting an unstructured discussion into a useful opportunity brief.

---

## Product Vision

Business conversations often begin with incomplete information, unclear requirements, and loosely described problems.

VantageBrief should create a more structured first interaction by helping the user:

1. Explain their business context
2. Identify the operational problem
3. Describe the current workflow
4. Clarify the tools and people involved
5. Define the desired outcome
6. Review practical recommendations
7. Receive a decision-ready summary by email

The product should make an early client conversation more useful for both the prospective client and the business receiving the enquiry.

---

## Product Positioning

VantageBrief should not be positioned as:

- A chatbot that sends email summaries
- A ChatGPT clone
- A generic lead-generation bot
- An automated questionnaire
- A replacement for professional consultation
- A guaranteed sales-conversion system

VantageBrief should be positioned as:

> An AI-guided discovery workspace that transforms business conversations into structured requirements, practical recommendations, and actionable opportunity briefs.

---

## Target Users

### Primary users

- Business owners exploring automation
- Operations managers facing repetitive workflows
- Teams considering an AI agent
- Prospective clients seeking custom software
- Consultants conducting early-stage discovery
- Technology agencies qualifying incoming opportunities

### Future Aginyx use

VantageBrief may later be integrated into the Aginyx website as its interactive client-discovery experience.

It should therefore support discussions around:

- AI agents
- Business process automation
- Internal knowledge assistants
- Customer-support automation
- Voice and conversational systems
- Full-stack AI products
- Custom software platforms
- Workflow integrations

The standalone product must not depend visually or technically on Aginyx branding during the first implementation.

---

## Core User Problem

Traditional website contact forms collect limited information.

They often provide only:

- Name
- Email
- Company
- A short message

This gives the receiving team little context and requires the same questions to be asked again during the first call.

VantageBrief should improve this experience by guiding the user through a focused conversation and organizing the information into a useful business brief.

---

## Core Business Value

VantageBrief is intended to support:

### Better context collection

The system helps users explain their challenge in greater detail than a standard form.

### More consistent discovery

Each conversation should progressively cover the information required to understand the opportunity.

### Faster preparation

A structured brief allows a business team to review the opportunity before a consultation.

### Improved user value

The user receives a useful summary and next-step recommendation instead of a generic submission confirmation.

### Clearer follow-up

The resulting brief should make later communication more focused and relevant.

No specific revenue, conversion, productivity, or cost-saving improvement may be claimed without real measured data.

---

## Product Principles

### Purpose before conversation

Every interaction should move the user toward clearer business understanding.

The product must not encourage endless or unrelated chatting.

### Progressive discovery

The system should collect information naturally over several messages instead of presenting a long rigid questionnaire.

### One useful question at a time

The AI should normally ask no more than one important follow-up question in a single response.

### Useful answers before further questions

When the user asks for advice, the AI should provide immediate value before asking for additional information.

### Clear distinction between facts and recommendations

The product must distinguish:

- Information provided by the user
- Information not yet known
- AI-generated recommendations
- Assumptions that require confirmation

### No fabricated completeness

Incomplete sections should remain visibly incomplete rather than being filled with invented information.

### Action only after explicit intent

The email automation should run only when the user explicitly asks for the brief to be emailed.

### Verified confirmation

The interface must confirm email success only after the backend reports that Gmail completed successfully.

---

# Primary Product Journey

## Stage 1: Product introduction

The landing page explains:

- What VantageBrief does
- Who it is designed for
- What business value it provides
- How the discovery process works
- What the user will receive

The user then selects the primary action:

```text
Start a Brief
```

The Phase 2 landing page contains:

- Hero with the focused product preview
- Core Business Value
- How It Works
- Use Cases
- Final CTA
- Footer

There is no separate Product Preview section. The hero contains the landing-page product preview.

The final landing navigation contains:

- How It Works
- Use Cases
- Start a Brief

About, Contact, Support, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Cookie Policy are deferred to Phase 7. Until those destinations exist, they must not link to empty or missing routes.
